\cvsection{Research Focus}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Evolutionary genomics, gene-family evolution, orthology, and duplicate-gene retention.
  \item Molecular adaptation, codon models, branch-site inference, and statistical identifiability.
  \item Multiple sequence alignment, learned residue-level scoring, and codon-aware analysis.
  \item Protein evolution, ancestral reconstruction, structural interpretation, and molecular function.
  \item Reproducible bioinformatics, sensitivity analysis, and evidence-aware omics research.
\end{itemize}

\cvsection{Research Software}
\textbf{BABAPPAlign}\par
Progressive multiple sequence alignment with learned residue-level scoring, fixed
protein-language-model embeddings, affine-gap dynamic programming, and codon-aware alignment.

\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{BABAPPASnake}\par
Reproducible workflow for orthogroup-centered episodic selection analysis, alternative
alignment pathways, phylogenetic inference, follow-up testing, and robustness summaries.
